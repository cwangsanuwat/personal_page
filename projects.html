Kidney Disease Genomic Variant Analysis and Interpretation (KidGeneVAI)

KidGeneVAI develops a standardized workflow to analyze and interpret genetic variants for patients with kidney disease in Thailand. The project connects genomic data with clinical interpretation to support individual reporting and research into the genetic landscape of kidney disease in Thai patients.
